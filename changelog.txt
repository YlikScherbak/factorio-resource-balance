Version: 1.7.0
Date: 2026-11-05
  Features:
    - Redesigned interface. The panel has flat rows with separators and short column headers; the last row is
      "+ Add resource" with a compact Σ toggle (count a new resource on all planets). Search moved to the title
      bar next to the help button.
    - Resource window with tabs: Chart, Producers & consumers, Root causes, Watcher. The title shows "+N" when
      resources are compared; the trash button removes the resource from the open list.
    - Producers & consumers starts with a diagnosis card: balance, producer history bars, output target verdict,
      possible cause, full storage and delivery hints in one place. Recipe shares are shown as bars.
    - Root causes ("Dig deeper") is a tab of the resource window now.
    - Watcher tab: the state with snooze and remove, thresholds with the current value next to each, the averaging
      window and the alert log of this resource. For a resource that is not in a list it offers to add it.
    - "Deficit" tab before the lists (replaces the "Negative only" mode): the 50 largest deficits on the planet,
      search finds the rest. "+" on a row opens an "Add to" window to choose the list.
    - One list dialog: "+" creates a list (optionally from an export string), the pencil edits the current one —
      name with an icon, export string, delete.
    - Chart marks are a collapsible panel in the chart tab ("Marks (N)"); it lists your marks and the watcher marks
      of the resources on this chart.
    - "Diagnostics" window: the factory idle view and the watcher log as two tabs. Idle has a bar of lost machine
      time per recipe.
  Changes:
    - The hotkey for adding what is under the cursor is Ctrl+Alt+R now (Alt+R conflicted with the NVIDIA overlay).
    - Recipe share is capped at 100% (an estimate above it is explained in the tooltip).
    - Empty states (no log yet, history still collecting) are shown as a centred message.
  Optimisations:
    - Much less GUI work per update: quality lookups are cached, mini-charts reuse their line segments, the
      Deficit tab draws at most 50 rows and recounts its number once a minute.
    - Producers & consumers takes machines from the background registry instead of searching the whole planet —
      no hitch when the tab opens on a large base.
  Bugfixes:
    - Multiplayer: the state of open windows is kept in the save, which could otherwise cause a desync.
    - English texts showed backslashes before quotes.

Version: 1.6.0
Date: 2026-11-05
  Features:
    - Machine state history: a background record of what every machine with a recipe was doing over the last
      30 minutes (map setting "Machine state history": every 5 / 10 / 30 s or off; the load is spread evenly over
      ticks). Producers & consumers shows "Producers over 10 min: working 54% · no input 31% · output full 12%",
      and the machine count of every recipe has the same breakdown in its tooltip.
    - "Idle" window: the recipes on the planet that stand still the most (lost machine time), the main reason and
      whether it hurts — the product's balance is red when it is short, grey when the machine just waits for demand.
    - Output target per resource (in the watcher dialog, now "Watcher and target"): "Produced" turns yellow below
      95% of the target; Producers & consumers gives a verdict — target reached (with capacity reserve), not enough
      machine capacity (with an estimate of how many machines to add), or enough capacity but machines lack input,
      have a full output or lack power.
    - "Dig deeper": a tree of possible root causes — follows short ingredients down the chain (up to 5 levels) and
      says at every branch end why it ends there: machines are working at full capacity, machines are stopped
      although ingredients are enough, no machines make it on the planet, storage is full.
    - Producers & consumers names the byproduct that clogs the output (or ash in a burner) — "1 of 9 producers
      are stopped: the output is clogged with X", whether X has no consumers, stopped consumers or working ones,
      with "Who consumes it" and "Show a machine" buttons. The blue status counter lists what clogs the output.
    - Full storage is recognised: when producers stand with a full output but consumers keep working (city blocks,
      trains), it is shown as "storage is full — not a deficit" instead of false cause and delivery warnings.
    - Fluid stock: the "Stock" column works for fluids too — fluid in storage tanks together with the pipes of
      their segments.
    - Watcher log ("Log" button): when each alert started and ended, how long it lasted, the lowest output and
      balance, and how it ended. Click a resource to open its chart.
    - Watcher marks on the chart: orange when a threshold breaks, green on recovery, grey when paused, snoozed or
      changed — only on that resource's chart (mod setting "Watcher: marks on the chart", on by default).
    - Alt+R adds what is under the cursor to the current list: an item, a fluid, a recipe's product, a machine's
      product, a drill's ore or an offshore pump's fluid (rebindable).
    - "Make a list from ingredients": a new list tab with the resource and the direct ingredients of the recipes
      that currently make it on the planet, with a preview to untick extras.
    - Icons in list tab names and chart marks (the icon selector button in the text field).
    - Mod setting: machine rescan interval for Producers & consumers — 10 / 30 / 60 s.
  Changes:
    - List tabs scroll horizontally, the panel no longer grows wider with many lists.
    - Esc closes the mod's dialogs one by one (watcher, marks, log, idle, lists…), then the chart window.
    - Cause and delivery hints no longer flicker when the balance hovers around zero.
    - Saving the watcher with unchanged thresholds keeps its state (no repeated alert).
    - Fluid stock in large bases is recounted in small portions over several ticks — no hitch.
  Bugfixes:
    - With quality (Space Age) only normal quality was counted: production, consumption, stock and the
      per-recipe breakdown now sum all qualities.
    - Machines that put their product straight onto a belt (e.g. Pyanodon's foundries) were shown as "other"
      instead of "output full"; frozen machines now count as "power or heat".
    - Removing a resource during a watcher alert left its log record "ongoing" forever.

Version: 1.5.0
Date: 2026-11-04
  Features:
    - Several lists: tabs above "My list" (up to 12) — e.g. one per planet or per production chain. New, rename
      and delete buttons; a tab shows the number of resources and a red status icon when its watcher fires.
    - Export / import a list as a text string — share it with friends or move it to another save
      (unknown resources from other mod sets are skipped, duplicates are not added twice).
    - The watcher pause is per list now: pause one tab while you rebuild, the others keep watching.
    - Producers & consumers tab: "Possible cause" hint for a resource in deficit — the ingredients of its main
      producers that are short on the same planet too. Click one to open its producers & consumers and follow
      the chain further.
    - Producers & consumers tab: "Check delivery" hint — when the resource is short but a noticeable part of its
      producers stand with a full output, the problem is in taking the product away (or a clogged byproduct),
      not in production.
    - Chart marks: note an event ("added a second iron line") at the current moment; it is shown on the chart
      of every resource as a dashed blue line with the text in the tooltip. Up to 50 marks.
  Changes:
    - Existing lists and the watcher pause move to the first tab ("Main") automatically.
    - Code split into modules (scripts/), no change in behaviour.

Version: 1.4.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Info:
    - Factorio 2.1 only from this version on (the last version for Factorio 2.0 is 1.3.0).
  Features:
    - Watcher: a bell button on every row of "My list" sets thresholds for the resource — minimum output and/or
      minimum balance per minute, checked against the average over 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes (production waves from
      trains and buffers don't trigger it). Catches a stopped line whose balance is 0 − 0 = 0.
    - A broken threshold turns the bell red and shows a counter on the mod button with the list of resources;
      you are notified once and again only after the resource recovers.
    - Mod settings (per player): "Watcher: chat message" and "Watcher: sound" (both off by default).
    - "Watcher: on / paused" button in the panel and "Snooze for 30 m" per resource — e.g. while you rebuild.
    - New "Stock" column: how long the stock in logistic networks lasts at the current deficit
      ("0" when there is none, "<1m", "≈15m", "≈3.5h"; red under 10 minutes, yellow under an hour).
    - Chart window: "What changed" table under the chart — the previous period against the last one
      (produced, consumed, balance and the change), plus a one-line verdict: whether production or
      consumption moved the balance, or that a line stopped completely.
    - Producers & consumers tab: machine status counters for every recipe — missing ingredients, output full,
      no power or fuel, other (a snapshot of the last scan, every 30 s, so the numbers don't flicker).
    - Click a status counter to jump to the next such machine on the map: remote view next to the window
      (not under it), the machine is circled in the colour of its status. Further clicks cycle through the machines.
  Changes:
    - The period-average line under the chart is replaced by the "What changed" table.
    - The chart background between stacked resources is filled (no dark blotches in Factorio 2.1).
    - The mod's internal chart surface is hidden from the remote view surface list.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Floating "Resource balance" panel: produced, consumed and balance per minute for chosen items and fluids.
    - Every row is bound to its own planet (with planet icon), so cross-planet dependencies stay visible.
    - Time periods like the vanilla statistics: 5s, 1m, 10m, 1h, 10h, 50h, 250h, 1000h.
    - "Negative only" mode: every item and fluid with a deficit on the current planet, largest first.
    - Search by translated or internal name, sorting by name, produced, consumed or balance.
    - Mini trend chart in every row.
    - Large chart window: thin production and consumption lines, balance band, grid and axis labels,
      hover tooltips with values at any moment, smoothing (Auto, None, Light, Strong).
    - Compare up to 4 resources on one chart with a shared time cursor (Ctrl+click or right-click a row).
    - "Producers & consumers" tab: which recipes make and use a resource, machine counts and shares,
      estimated instantly from game statistics or measured live from machines; buildings grouped separately.
    - Open with Ctrl+Shift+R, the shortcut bar button or the top-left button.
  Locale:
    - English, Ukrainian, Russian.
